01 / A little about me

A thoughtful mind.
A versatile skill set.

Organised by nature, technical by training, and comfortable working wherever people, paperwork and platforms meet.

I studied Computer Science at Veritas University, Abuja, and graduated on 23 July 2026 with Second Class Upper (2:1). Alongside my degree, I supported lecturers with scheduling and records, helped coordinate SIWES administration, and handled official communication for student bodies.

My work has spanned office administration, travel documentation and client support at a travel agency, as well as social media management for a beauty brand, a TEDx event and a student community. These roles called for adapting quickly, keeping records accurate, following up reliably, and making sure a brand’s communication looks and sounds consistent.

My technical background shapes how I work. I’m comfortable with spreadsheets and documentation, understand how web applications are put together, and have worked on projects using Python, Flask, React and MongoDB. I also design interfaces in Figma, social graphics in Canva and short videos in CapCut, allowing me to move between operational and digital work.

My strengths are adaptability—moving between administration, client work and digital projects; organisation—keeping clear records and dependable schedules; communication—writing professional correspondence and timely follow-ups; problem-solving—applying a Computer Science mindset to everyday operational questions; and digital skills—from spreadsheets and design tools to web technologies and social platforms.
